The **Rainbow GP** is on! Choose from *8 unicorns* and race through *6 unique tracks* that will test your skills. Show the mythical realm that you've got what it takes to be the champion!

## Controls
### In-Game
* **Move**: <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> or <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd>
* **Gallop**: Tap <kbd>K</kbd> as fast as you can

### Main Menu
* **Move**: <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> or <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd>
* **Start Game**: Press <kbd>K</kbd>

## Meet the Racers
* **Rufus**: The red unicorn. This is his first time participating in the Rainbow GP, which has been his dream since he was a colt. He is highly hyperactive.
* **Bowie**: The yellow unicorn. Rufus's best friend, who is quite artistic. Unlike Rufus, he is more calm and easygoing.
* **Ewan**: The green unicorn. He and Bowie debuted in the Rainbow GP during the same season and have been rivals ever since. It must be said that Ewan is much louder about their rivalry.
* **Skye**: The blue unicorn. She is Ewan’s sister and likes to excel at everything she does. Some might say she always looks serious.
* **Ailish**: The purple unicorn. She comes from a noble family of unicorns. Like Rufus, Ailish is quite bubbly.
* **Heather**: The pink unicorn. She is Ailish's cousin and a friend of Skye. Rufus has feelings for Heather, but it remains to be seen if they are reciprocated. She is very feminine.
* **Iris**: The white unicorn. She has been participating in the Rainbow GP for a long time. She is very caring toward the younger unicorns who join the fray.
* **Volker**: The black unicorn. He is the oldest racer. He comes from a family of champions, and while he was once a champion himself, he hasn't been able to come out on top lately. Will this season be any different?

## Developer's Notes
This game is heavily inspired by *F-Zero* for the SNES, and it was developed as an exercise to implement a "Mode 7"-like renderer using the Canvas API. While a lot of features were originally planned, they had to be cut due to time and space constraints for this year's js13kGames. That said, I'm quite pleased with the result and I'm looking forward to implementing a few improvements in the future (such as using Web Workers to improve rendering performance and adding mobile support).
